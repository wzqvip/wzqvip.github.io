来美国的第一个工作日。 F-1学生身份，无SSN/ITIN。

## Chase: $125

[https://account.chase.com/consumer/banking/studentLPchampion](https://account.chase.com/consumer/banking/studentLPchampion)

-   1 Open a new Chase College Checking account online or with your coupon at a [local branch](https://www.chase.com/meeting-scheduler/getstarted).

-   2 Complete 10 qualifying transactions within 60 days of coupon enrollment.[1](https://account.chase.com/consumer/banking/studentLPchampion#note1)

这个简单，开户之后交易10笔，包括消费和转账。

## huntington: $400

[https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers)

**Perks Checking**[†](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers#%E2%80%A0 "View Interest Rate Disclosure")

**Earn $400 when you open a new Perks Checking account and set up and receive total qualifying direct deposits of $500 or more within 90 days of account opening and keep account open for 90 days**[¶](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers#%C2%B6 "View Checking Bonus Offer Disclosure")

**Platinum Perks Checking**[†](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers#%E2%80%A0 "View Interest Rate Disclosure")

**Earn $600 when you open a new Platinum Perks Checking account and make total new money deposits of $25,000 or more within 90 days of account opening and keep account open for 90 days**[¶](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers#%C2%B6 "View Checking Bonus Offer Disclosure")

-   No monthly maintenance fee with $1,000 in total monthly deposits, or $5,000 in total relationship balances, otherwise $10 a month[^](https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers#^ "View Total Relationship Balances Disclosure")

这里选择$400， 要求90天内 dd 500及以上。 以及要注意每月入账1k或者5k存款来减免月费。 前两个月没月份。 需要账户开满三个月并且满足条件，之后14天内会发放奖励。到账之后可以转成普通账户无月费。

这个似乎取决于网点，网上没有公共的promo [![](Screenshot-2025-08-18-184939-155x300.png)](Screenshot-2025-08-18-184939.png) 要求：
