[俄亥俄] 美卡小记 - F1学生篇

来美国的第一个工作日。 F-1学生身份，无SSN/ITIN。

Chase: $125

https://account.chase.com/consumer/banking/studentLPchampion

  • 1 Open a new Chase College Checking account online or with your coupon at a local branch.

  • 2 Complete 10 qualifying transactions within 60 days of coupon enrollment.1

这个简单，开户之后交易10笔，包括消费和转账。

huntington: $400

https://www.huntington.com/checking-account-promotions-bonuses-offers

Perks Checking†

Earn $400 when you open a new Perks Checking account and set up and receive total qualifying direct deposits of $500 or more within 90 days of account opening and keep account open for 90 days¶

Platinum Perks Checking†

Earn $600 when you open a new Platinum Perks Checking account and make total new money deposits of $25,000 or more within 90 days of account opening and keep account open for 90 days¶

  • No monthly maintenance fee with $1,000 in total monthly deposits, or $5,000 in total relationship balances, otherwise $10 a month^

这里选择$400， 要求90天内 dd 500及以上。 以及要注意每月入账1k或者5k存款来减免月费。 前两个月没月份。 需要账户开满三个月并且满足条件，之后14天内会发放奖励。到账之后可以转成普通账户无月费。

US Bank: $100

这个似乎取决于网点，网上没有公共的promo 要求：

  • 存入1k
  • 10笔消费，总额100以上
  • 启用线上银行/手机银行

难度也不大。 主要是25岁以下学生每月免一次电汇接收费用。
