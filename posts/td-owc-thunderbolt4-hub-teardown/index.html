[TD] OWC Thunderbolt4 Hub Teardown

OWC Thunderbolt Hub with Three Thunderbolt 4 Ports and One USB Port

PCB

PCB1-Front PCB1-Back PCB2-Front

Chips

Intel® JHL8440 Thunderbolt™ 4 Controller

CYPD5235: USB Type-C Port Controller Parade FL5801: 5-Port USB2 Port Expander for TBT4 Docks Note: JHL8440 only extends THUNDERBOLT at all ports. Add this to support USB2.0 feature. infineon CY7C6521: USB-Serial Dual Channel (UART/I2C/SPI) Bridge with CAPSENSE™ and BCD TI MSP430FR2110: 16 MHz MCU with 2KB FRAM, 1KB SRAM, comparator, 10-bit ADC, UART/SPI, timer. Note: Interact with JHL8440, BLUE LED for CONNECTED, WHITE for STANDBY.

Notes

Intel didn't make full pcie3.0x4 thunderbolt 4 controllers. So there's only TBT3/USB4/TBT5 SSD/GPU docks:

  • TBT3 ~3100MBps
  • USB4 ~3500MBps
  • TBT5 ~6400MBps

For Thunderbolt 4 Dock with SSD/PCIE support, needs to be JHL8440 TBT HUB + JHL 7440 TBT3 controller. Expensive&sucks. JHL7440 TBT3 controller: TBT daisy chain, USB3 10Gbps, PCIE3.0x4, 4xHBR2 DP
